# Meta (Facebook / Instagram) Campaign — Draft

**Objective:** Own the near-empty IG/FB lane (only Nour, ~1 live ad). Build a buyer DM/lead list and a retargeting pool.
**Structure:** 3 campaigns →
1. **Engagement/Lead (cold)** — the lead magnet
2. **Awareness/Video (cold)** — authority + trust
3. **Retargeting (warm)** — closers

**Placements:** Advantage+ (IG-first — Nour proves IG is the channel here). **Format:** vertical 9:16 video/reels primary.
**Geo:** Indonesia + `[Australia, Singapore, UK, Germany, Netherlands, USA]`.
**Audiences:** Interests — real estate investing, overseas property, Bali/Lombok travel, expat/digital nomad, surfing; Lookalike of site visitors + WhatsApp leads once seeded.

## Compliance note
Real-estate ads can trigger Meta's **Special Ad Category (Housing)** in some regions — if prompted, set the category, which limits some targeting (age/gender/detailed geo). Plan audiences accordingly.

# 05 — Opportunities & Counter-Ad Playbook

**For:** Razzaque Paradise Properties (propertieslombok.com)
**Based on:** the competitor scan in this folder (2026-06-10)

The paid arena in your niche is unusually open: **one active rival (Nour), one paused (SeaScape), two not playing (Discover, Maju).** Below is what to exploit and the exact ads to run.

---

## The five gaps you can own

| # | Gap | Evidence | Your move |

## Channel plan

### A. Google Search (highest-intent, least contested — start here)
Target the geo + product keywords competitors have vacated. Suggested ad set:

**Ad 1 — Land, Kuta/South Lombok**
- Headlines: `Land for Sale in Kuta Lombok` · `Verified Titles, Clear Due Diligence` · `From $XXk — Talk on WhatsApp`
- Description: *"Freehold & leasehold land in South Lombok, vetted titles and full legal guidance. See live listings and prices. Personal tours available."*
- Sitelinks: Villas for Sale · Land for Sale · Sell Your Property · Book a Tour

**Ad 2 — Villas / investment**
- Headlines: `Lombok Villas for Sale` · `8–12% Target Rental Yields` · `Full-Service, From Search to Title`
- Description: *"Curated villas in South Lombok with real numbers, not guesses. Tours, negotiation and legal handled end-to-end."*

**Ad 3 — Beat SeaScape's vacated USP**
- Headline: `Interactive Lombok Property Map` · `Browse Villas & Land by Location`

> Add **price extensions** (Nour uses "from $65K") and a **WhatsApp/call CTA** — both proven in this market.

### B. Meta / Instagram (own the empty lane)
1. **Lead-magnet ad (copy Nour's best workhorse, do it better):**
   > "Buying land or a villa in Lombok? Avoid the 7 mistakes that cost foreign buyers the most — fees, fake titles, zoning traps. Comment **'GUIDE'** (or DM us) for our free Lombok Buyer's Checklist. 🌴"
   - Mechanic: comment-to-DM + retarget commenters. Build the list.
2. **Hard-numbers authority video** (their strongest format): "South Lombok by the numbers 2026" — your own version with current occupancy/yield/appreciation stats and *your* verified listings.
3. **Trust/legal explainer:** "Leasehold vs Freehold in Lombok, explained in 60 seconds" — owns the fear nobody addresses.
4. **Testimonial / walkthrough reels** — your service-led brand + real client stories beats Nour's thin social proof (52 likes / 4k IG).

### C. Lead-gen assets to build (they double as ad hooks)
- **Free ROI / rental-yield calculator** on the site → ad hook "Stop guessing. Calculate your Lombok ROI free."
- **Free Lombok Buyer's Checklist / mini-guide** → the comment/DM lead magnet.
- **Interactive listings map** → reclaim SeaScape's angle.

### D. Cheap top-of-funnel (steal Nour's content-ad trick)
Run low-CPC content ads on **travel/research intent** — "Best Beaches in South Lombok", "Is Lombok the next Bali?", "Things to do in Kuta Lombok" — then **retarget** those visitors with property + investment ads.

## 30-day priority order

1. **Week 1:** Stand up Google Search (Ads 1–3) on the uncontested geo keywords + WhatsApp CTA. Fastest ROI.
2. **Week 1–2:** Launch the Meta lead-magnet ad (comment "GUIDE") + build the buyer's checklist PDF.
3. **Week 2–3:** Publish the hard-numbers video + a leasehold-vs-freehold explainer; set up retargeting.
4. **Week 3–4:** Ship the ROI calculator and interactive map; turn both into ad hooks.
5. **Ongoing:** Re-scan competitors monthly (see [`04-inactive-competitors.md`](./04-inactive-competitors.md) watch list). Watch for Nour scaling Meta or Discover/Maju entering paid.

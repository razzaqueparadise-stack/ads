# 03 — Deep Dive: SeaScape Lombok

> **Legal name (Google):** SeaScape Lombok Properties
> **Web:** seascapelombok.com · **FB:** facebook.com/SeaScapeLombok
> **Status:** Paid ads **PAUSED** — 10 Google search ads, all inactive, last live early Feb 2026. No active Meta ads.

SeaScape ran a focused **Google Search–only** campaign for ~14 months (longest creative ran 426 days) targeting high-intent Kuta-Lombok villa & land buyers, then stopped. Their whole differentiator was an **interactive property map** — and since they've gone dark, that angle is currently unclaimed.

## Patterns

- **Channel:** Google Search exclusively. No display, no video, no Meta.
- **Keywords targeted:** "Villas For Sale Kuta Lombok", "Land For Sale Kuta Lombok", "Beachfront Plot", "Turnkey Project", "off plan villas South Lombok".
- **USP / hook (repeated):** the **"interactive property map"** — "Search our interactive map to navigate the diverse Real Estate opportunities."
- **Product framing:** off-plan + luxury villas + land parcels + turnkey projects.
- **CTA:** "connect with our agents", Contact sitelinks.
- **Tone:** lifestyle-leaning ("Find Your Sea Side Escape") more than Nour's hard-numbers investment tone.

## What to take from SeaScape

1. **The interactive map is up for grabs.** It was their entire differentiator and they've stopped advertising it. An interactive map of Lombok listings on propertieslombok.com is a strong, ownable angle.
2. **Their keyword set is now uncontested on Search** — "Villas/Land For Sale Kuta Lombok", "beachfront plot", "turnkey project". You can rank without fighting them.
3. **Use rich sitelinks** like they did (Villas For Sale · Land For Sale · Villas Collection · Contact) — it widens your SERP footprint and lifts CTR.
4. **They never ran Meta or video** — a coordinated multi-channel push beats their search-only approach.

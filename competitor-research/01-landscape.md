# 01 — The Competitive Landscape

**Market:** Lombok (esp. South Lombok / Kuta–Mandalika / Selong Belanak) villa & land sales to foreign and domestic investors.

## Your position

**Razzaque Paradise Properties** (propertieslombok.com) — "Opening Doors to Your Future." Curated villa, land and home listings across Lombok with search/filter tools, in-person & virtual tours, buy-side and sell-side brokerage, and legal/negotiation guidance. Positioning is **service- and trust-led** (testimonials, local knowledge, secure transactions).

## What the market is selling on

Across every competitor that advertises, the message converges on a small set of levers:

1. **The investment / yield story** — "passive rental income", "high ROI", "8–12% gross yields", "from $65K". Property is framed as a **financial instrument**, not a home.
2. **South Lombok vs Bali** — the recurring narrative is "Bali is saturated, South Lombok is the next wave." Nour leans on this hardest.
3. **Geography-specific intent** — Kuta Lombok, Selong Belanak, Mandalika, beachfront plots. These are the high-intent search terms.
4. **Trust & legality** — AREBI membership, due-diligence, legal transparency. Important because foreign buyers fear leasehold/freehold pitfalls.
5. **Low-friction contact** — WhatsApp, DM, "Contact Us", comment-to-DM lead magnets.

## Strategic read

- The **paid-search lane is barely contested**: of four rivals only Nour is live on Google today, and SeaScape (paused) is the only other that ever ran search ads. Discover & Maju are absent.
- The **Meta/Instagram lane is almost empty**: only Nour, and only with a single active creative at a time.
- The **content-marketing-as-ads** tactic (running "best beaches" / "7-day itinerary" articles as ads to capture tourists, then retargeting) is used by **only Nour** — a repeatable edge that's cheap to copy.
- Differentiators on the table that nobody currently owns: an **interactive map** (SeaScape built one then went dark), **video walkthroughs**, and **transparent legal/process education** for nervous foreign buyers.

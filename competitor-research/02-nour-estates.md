## C. What to take from Nour Estates

1. **Copy the "7 mistakes" lead-magnet mechanic** — it's their year-long workhorse. A "comment GUIDE / DM for the free Lombok buyer's checklist" ad is cheap and list-building.
2. **Build a free ROI calculator** on propertieslombok.com — it's both a lead-gen tool and an ad hook ("Stop guessing. Start calculating.").
3. **Lead with hard numbers** — they quote 8–12% yields, 15–20% land appreciation, 67% occupancy. Vague "villas for sale" can't compete with specifics.
4. **Run content ads on tourist keywords** ("best beaches in Lombok", "things to do in Lombok") and retarget — a proven, low-CPC top-of-funnel.
5. **Their weakness = thin social proof** (52 page likes, 4k IG). Out-produce them on testimonials, video walkthroughs, and follower growth and you out-trust them.

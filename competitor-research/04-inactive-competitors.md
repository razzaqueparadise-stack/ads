## Discover Lombok Property

> **Web:** discoverlombokproperty.com · **FB:** facebook.com/discoverlombokproperty · **IG:** @discoverlombokproperty · **YouTube:** @discoverlombokproperty
> **Base:** Kuta Mandalika, Lombok

- **Paid ads:** None found — no active Meta ads, no Google Transparency advertiser record.
- **Positioning:** "Premium Land and Villas for Sale" in South Lombok; contemporary villa designs; experienced legal services; acts as both **agent and developer**.
- **Channel strategy:** Clearly **content/SEO + YouTube + organic social** driven (active blog: "Living the Digital Nomad Dream in Lombok", news archive). They invest in owned media, not paid.
- **Implication for you:** They will rank organically for "Lombok property" terms over time. To compete in the short term, **paid search lets you leapfrog them** on the exact keywords they're earning slowly. Long term, you'll want your own content engine to match.

## Maju Properties

> **Web:** majuproperties.com
> **Base:** Kuta, South Lombok

- **Paid ads:** None found — no Facebook ad page surfaced under the name, no Google Transparency advertiser record.
- **Positioning:** Boutique, "small, passionate team offering a fresh perspective on property investment in South Lombok"; also offers **property management** (recurring-revenue angle, useful for after-sale retention).
